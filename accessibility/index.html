Accessibility statement

Spaces wants every apartment owner to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities.

Standard we follow

We build this site to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. That covers the Israeli Standard IS 5568 at level AA, referenced by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and the European standard EN 301 549, referenced by Portuguese Decreto-Lei 82/2022.

Conformance status

Partially conformant. The content Spaces controls meets the standard according to our latest assessment. Some third-party content does not fully meet it; it is listed below with an alternative.

What we have done

  • Full keyboard navigation, a skip to content link and a visible focus indicator on every control.
  • A logical heading structure, labelled page regions and text alternatives for meaningful images. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Colour contrast at or above WCAG AA, including text over photographs and form field borders.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200%, and the layout reflows down to 320 pixels wide without horizontal scrolling.
  • The background video on the home page has no sound, can be paused with a button, and stays still when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • The income estimator works with Tab and the arrow keys, and updated results are announced to screen readers.
  • The contact form has visible labels and marks required fields. Errors are shown in text, announced, and focus moves to the first field that needs attention.
  • An accessibility menu, at the bottom left of each page, lets you adjust text size, contrast, spacing, font, link highlighting and animations. It is an extra: the site is built to be accessible without it. Its settings are saved in your browser only after you change one.

Known limitations and alternatives

  • Map. The embedded map is provided by Google and may not be fully accessible. The area we cover, Porto and Vila Nova de Gaia, is also written on the page, and a link opens the area in Google Maps.
  • Terms and conditions. The terms are PDF documents hosted on spaces-pt.com and have not yet been checked for accessibility. Email us and we will send the content in an accessible format.
  • WhatsApp. The contact form can open WhatsApp, an external service. You can choose Send by email, or call us, instead.

How we assessed this site

Assessed on 22 November 2026 with automated testing (axe-core, WCAG 2.0 to 2.2, levels A and AA) on desktop and at 320 pixels wide, and manual checks of keyboard navigation, focus visibility, zoom to 200%, text spacing, colour contrast over images and form error handling.
