A good manager can make an apartment more profitable and much less stressful. A poor fit can do the opposite. These questions help you compare offers on equal terms.

## Money

1. **What is the fee, and is IVA included?** In Portugal, 23% IVA on a management fee is a meaningful difference. Spaces' short-stay fee, for example, is quoted as 20% of booking income plus IVA.
2. **What is the fee calculated on?** Gross booking income, income after platform commission, or something else?
3. **Which costs are passed on to me?** Cleaning, linen, consumables, maintenance call-outs, photography, onboarding.
4. **How and when am I paid?** Monthly transfers, which account, and what the statement shows.

## Service

5. **What exactly is included?** Guest messages, check-in, cleaning, linen, listing management, pricing, maintenance coordination.
6. **Who answers guests, and when?** Including nights, weekends and in which languages.
7. **How are repairs handled?** Is there a spending limit before they ask you, and do they mark up contractor invoices?

## Compliance

8. **Who handles [tourist tax](/blog/tourist-tax-porto-gaia) collection and payment, and foreign guest registration?**
9. **Who is responsible for the [AL registration](/blog/alojamento-local-licence-porto-2026) staying valid,** including insurance renewals and council requirements?

## Control and contract

10. **What reports do I get, and how often?** Bookings, occupancy, income, costs and reviews.
11. **Can I see the calendar and block dates for my own use?**
12. **How long is the contract, and how do I leave?** Notice period, fees on exit, and what happens to future bookings and the listing.

## Red flags

- Income promises without showing how they were calculated.
- Vague answers on what is included.
- Long lock-in periods with exit penalties.
- No written monthly reporting.
