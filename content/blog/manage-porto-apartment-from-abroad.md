Many of the owners Spaces works with live outside Portugal. Owning from abroad works well when the paperwork is set up once, properly, and day-to-day operations are handled locally.

## The paperwork checklist

1. **NIF (Portuguese tax number).** Needed to buy property, sign contracts, open a bank account and declare income.
2. **Tax notifications.** Decide how the tax authority will reach you. Official guidance says EU and EEA residents can choose electronic notifications instead of a tax representative. If you live outside the EU and EEA, for example in Israel, the UK or the US, confirm with a Portuguese accountant whether you must appoint a tax representative for your situation.
3. **Portuguese bank account.** Simplifies paying utilities, condominium fees and taxes, and receiving payouts.
4. **Certified accountant.** Needed for short-stay (Alojamento Local) income, and useful for any rental income. See our [tax primer](/blog/rental-income-tax-portugal-owners).
5. **Insurance.** Building and contents cover, plus civil liability insurance if the flat is used for short stays.
6. **Utilities and internet** in your name, with direct debit, so nothing is cut off while you are away.
7. **Condominium.** Know who the administrator is and keep up with fees and building decisions.
8. **Power of attorney** for a lawyer or trusted person, if you expect to need signatures while abroad.

## Taxes in two countries

Rental income from a Portuguese property is normally taxed in Portugal. Your home country may tax it as well and give credit for the Portuguese tax under a double tax treaty. Portugal and Israel have had such a treaty in force since 2008. Ask advisers in both countries to coordinate, ideally before the first booking.

## Operations on the ground

Paperwork is the easy part. What usually goes wrong for absent owners is operational:

- **Keys and access.** Who lets guests or tenants in, and who has spare keys.
- **Cleaning and linen** between stays, to a consistent standard.
- **Repairs.** A leak or a broken boiler needs someone local within hours, not days.
- **Guest communication** at all hours, in several languages.
- **Compliance.** [Tourist tax](/blog/tourist-tax-porto-gaia), guest registration and insurance renewals.

This is the part a property manager takes over. With Spaces, guest messages, cleaning, check-in, listings and small repairs are handled by the team in Porto, and you are kept informed.

## Staying in control

Agree in writing how you will be informed: a monthly statement of bookings, income and costs, and immediate contact for urgent issues. Make sure you can see the calendar and block dates for your own visits. Good management from abroad should feel boring: predictable reports, no surprises.
