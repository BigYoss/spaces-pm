An apartment that sits empty earns nothing, and a dated one earns less than it could. Preparing a flat to rent is part compliance, part comfort and part presentation.

## 1. What the law requires (Alojamento Local)

For short stays, the flat must meet the [Alojamento Local](/blog/alojamento-local-licence-porto-2026) requirements before guests arrive. Current guides and council information include:

- Fire extinguisher, accessible to guests
- Fire blanket in the kitchen
- First aid kit
- The 112 emergency number visible
- Gas detector, where the installation requires it
- Complaints book (Livro de Reclamações), with access to the electronic version
- Information for guests about the flat, the building and emergencies, in more than one language
- Civil liability insurance for guests and third parties (current guides cite a minimum of €75,000 per claim)

Requirements can differ by council and change over time. Confirm the list when you register.

## 2. The essentials guests judge

- **Sleep.** A good mattress, blackout curtains or blinds, quality linen and two sets per bed.
- **Kitchen.** Hob, oven or combination microwave, fridge, kettle, coffee machine and enough crockery for the maximum number of guests.
- **Bathroom.** Good water pressure and hot water, towels per guest, a hairdryer.
- **Work and connectivity.** Fast, reliable Wi-Fi and somewhere to sit and work, which matters even more for medium stays.
- **Climate.** Porto winters are damp. Heating and a dehumidifier make a difference to reviews.
- **Storage.** Space to unpack, hangers, a luggage spot.
- **Safety and access.** Working locks, a clear check-in method and a key plan.

## 3. Design for the guest you want

A flat for city-break couples is set up differently from one for a relocating family or a remote worker. Decide who you are designing for (our [rental model comparison](/blog/short-medium-long-term-rental-porto) helps), then furnish for them: durable materials, easy-to-clean surfaces, enough lighting, and a few local touches. Avoid personal items and clutter.

## 4. Photos and listing

The listing is the shop window. Professional photos in daylight, a clear floor plan of what is included and an honest description set the right expectations and reduce complaints.
