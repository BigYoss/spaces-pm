Tax is the part of renting in Portugal that owners most often underestimate. This primer explains the main ideas so you can have a better conversation with your accountant. It is not a substitute for one.

## Short stays: category B income

Income from Alojamento Local is generally treated as a business activity, which in Portuguese income tax (IRS) is called category B. Most individual owners use the **simplified regime**, where tax is calculated on a fixed share of gross income instead of on actual profit.

| Type of AL | Share of income that is taxed |
|---|---|
| Flat or house outside a containment area | 35% |
| Flat or house inside a containment area | 50% (from the 2026 State Budget, according to current tax guides) |
| Rooms or guesthouse-style accommodation | 15% |

The taxed share is then added to your other Portuguese income and taxed at your IRS rates. Two practical points:

- **The 15% rule.** You must be able to document expenses equal to at least 15% of gross income. Keep every invoice.
- **Organised accounts.** Some owners choose full accounting instead of the simplified regime, or hold the property in a company. Which is better depends on your numbers; ask your accountant to compare.

## Long-term rents: category F income

Rent from a tenant who lives in the flat is property income, category F. The base is a special flat rate of **25%**, and owners can choose to add it to their other income instead. Since 2023, long contracts benefit from reductions to that rate, larger for longer contracts, with conditions on the rent level for newer contracts. The rules for moderate rents changed again in 2026, so ask your accountant which rate applies to your specific contract.

## Medium stays

Stays of a few weeks or months, for example for remote workers or students, are usually made under a fixed-term rental contract rather than AL. How that income is taxed depends on the contract, so set it up with your accountant before the first tenant arrives.

## Other taxes to keep in mind

- **IMI**, the annual municipal property tax, is due whatever the rental model.
- **Tourist tax** is collected from guests on short stays. See [our guide to the tourist tax in Porto and Gaia](/blog/tourist-tax-porto-gaia).
- **VAT (IVA)** rules for AL depend on your turnover and setup. Spaces' own management fee is charged with IVA.

## If you live abroad

Portuguese rental income is normally taxed in Portugal, where the property is. Your country of residence may also tax it and give credit for the Portuguese tax under a double tax treaty. Portugal has a treaty with Israel, in force since 2008. You may also need a Portuguese tax representative; [our guide to managing an apartment from abroad](/blog/manage-porto-apartment-from-abroad) covers that.
