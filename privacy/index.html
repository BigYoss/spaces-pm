Is giving us your data required?

No law requires you to give us these details. Without a name and a way to reach you we cannot reply to your enquiry.

Who receives it

  • Vercel Inc. hosts this website (United States, certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.) if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd.) provides our email service (Gmail).
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd.) only if you choose Show map.
  • Members of the Spaces team in Israel who handle owner consultations. Israel is recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate protection.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tags, so it shows no cookie banner.

  • Map. The map in the contact section stays off until you choose Show map. Google Maps may then set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Accessibility menu. Stores your display settings in your browser only after you change one. This is strictly necessary to provide what you asked for.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and spaces-pt.com take you to those services, which have their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw any consent you gave. Under Israeli law you can also ask to review and correct information held about you. Write to team@spaces-pt.com. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Portugal the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), in Israel the Privacy Protection Authority (gov.il).

Security and children

The website is served only over HTTPS and holds no database of visitors. Our services are for property owners and not aimed at children under 16.
